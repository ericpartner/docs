exIQtive is built from modules. Each module covers one part of making your organization ready, and the modules work together. Modules sit under five pillars: **Clarify**, **Certify**, **Pay Fairly**, **Engage** and **Synchronize**.

Some modules are available today. The others are marked <Badge color="gray">Coming soon</Badge>.

## Always available

### Organization

Model your organization: [employees](/organization/employees), [departments](/organization/departments), [positions](/organization/positions), [roles](/organization/roles), [responsibilities](/responsibilities/catalog), [job families](/organization/job-families) and [job categories](/organization/job-categories). Every other module builds on it.

### IQ Agent

[IQ](/iq/ask-iq), the organizational intelligence agent, works in every module. IQ suggests and a person decides.

## Clarify

Turn strategy into goals, KPIs and responsibilities. See [Clarify](/concepts/clarify).

### Performance <Badge color="gray">Coming soon</Badge>

Set goals and KPIs, build scorecards, and track results every period.

### Procedures <Badge color="gray">Coming soon</Badge>

Document the standard operating procedures your teams follow.

## Certify

Prove that employees are ready for their responsibilities. See [Certify](/concepts/certify).

### Readiness

Coaches inspect employees against [checkpoints](/checkpoints/overview) and issue [badges](/user-guide/readiness/badge-ladder). Each badge carries a percentage, and the percentages roll up into [readiness](/user-guide/readiness/how-readiness-is-calculated) for each employee, position and department.

### Knowledge <Badge color="gray">Coming soon</Badge>

Capture the versioned videos, articles and quizzes that checkpoints draw on.
