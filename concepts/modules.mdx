exIQtive.com is built from modules. Each module covers one part of making your organization ready, and the modules work together. Both modules build on your [organization](/organization/understanding-your-organization): the employees, departments, positions, roles and responsibilities you model in exIQtive.com.

## The modules

| Module | What it does | Step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Readiness](/readiness/overview) | Prove employees are ready for their responsibilities. | [Certify](/concepts/certify) |
| [Knowledge](/knowledge/overview) | Capture the videos, articles and quizzes that checkpoints draw on. | [Certify](/concepts/certify) |

To see every feature in each module and which ones need the other module, see [Module features and dependencies](/concepts/module-features).

## Readiness

Coaches inspect employees against [checkpoints](/checkpoints/overview) and issue [badges](/user-guide/readiness/badge-ladder). Each badge carries a percentage, and the percentages roll up into [readiness](/user-guide/readiness/how-readiness-is-calculated) for each employee, position and department.

## Knowledge

Capture the videos, articles and quizzes that checkpoints draw on. See [Knowledge](/knowledge/overview).

## Your organization

Model your organization: [employees](/organization/employees), [departments](/organization/departments), [positions](/organization/positions), [roles](/organization/roles), and [responsibilities](/responsibilities/catalog). Readiness and Knowledge both build on it, and it is included with every account.

## IQ works across all modules

[IQ](/iq/ask-iq), the organizational intelligence agent, works in every module. IQ suggests and a person decides.

## How modules relate to the method

The modules serve the Certify step of [the exIQtive method](/concepts/method). The method explains why. The modules are the parts of exIQtive.com that do the work.
