The exIQtive method turns strategy into behavior, at scale. It has five pillars. Four of them form a cycle, and the fifth keeps the cycle turning:

- **Clarify**, **Certify**, **Pay fairly** and **Engage** are the four steps.
- **Synchronize** is the outer ring. Disciplined rituals keep every step current, so the organization doesn't drift.

Every module in exIQtive sits under one of these pillars. [IQ](/iq/ask-iq), the organizational intelligence agent, works across all five.

## The five pillars

| Pillar | The question it answers | Modules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Clarify](/concepts/clarify) | What does each employee need to do, and why? | Performance, Procedures |
| [Certify](/concepts/certify) | Is each employee ready to do it? | Readiness, Knowledge |
| [Pay fairly](/concepts/pay-fairly) | Is each employee paid fairly for the work and the results? | Fair Pay, Fair Bonuses |
| [Engage](/concepts/engage) | Is each employee in the right job, managed in the right way, and engaged? | Engagement |
| [Synchronize](/concepts/synchronize) | Is the whole organization working to the same rhythm? | Rituals |

Some modules are available today and others are marked <Badge>Coming soon</Badge>. See [Modules](/concepts/modules) for what's available.

## How the pillars connect

Each pillar builds on the one before it:

1. **Clarify** gives every employee a clear strategy, goals, and responsibilities.
2. **Certify** proves each employee is ready to perform those responsibilities, and turns readiness into a number.
3. **Pay fairly** uses that readiness, together with company results, to calculate base pay and bonus.
4. **Engage** checks that people fit their jobs and measures how they feel about their work.
5. **Synchronize** repeats all of it on a schedule, so every pillar stays current.
