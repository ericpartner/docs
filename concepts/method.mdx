The exIQtive method turns strategy into behavior, at scale. It has five steps. Four of them form a cycle, and the fifth keeps the cycle turning:

- **Clarify**, **Certify**, **Pay fairly** and **Engage** are the first four steps.
- **Synchronize** is the fifth step and the outer ring. Disciplined rituals keep every step current, so the organization doesn't drift.

Every module in exIQtive.com sits under one of these steps. [IQ](/iq/ask-iq), the organizational intelligence agent, works across all five.

## How the steps connect

Each step builds on the one before it:

1. **Clarify** gives every employee a clear strategy, goals, and responsibilities.
2. **Certify** proves each employee is ready to perform those responsibilities, and turns readiness into a number.
3. **Pay fairly** uses that readiness, together with company results, to calculate base pay and bonus.
4. **Engage** checks that people fit their jobs and measures how they feel about their work.
5. **Synchronize** repeats all of it on a schedule, so every step stays current.
