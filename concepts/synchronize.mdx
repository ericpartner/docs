Synchronize is the fifth pillar of the exIQtive method, and the outer ring that keeps the other four turning. Clarify, Certify, Pay fairly and Engage all decay without a rhythm. Synchronize gives them one through **disciplined rituals**.

  Synchronize isn't available in exIQtive.com yet <Badge>Coming soon</Badge>. This page explains the thinking behind Synchronize.
</Note>

## Discipline beats motivation

Motivation peaks and fades. Discipline gives you steady momentum. A ritual turns discipline into something a team does without relying on anyone's mood.

## A ritual is not a meeting

| Meeting | Ritual |
| --- | --- |
| No clear purpose | Clear intent |
| Improvised | A script |
| When needed | A cadence |

### Three rules of a ritual

1. **Share the page 24 hours before.** Everyone gets the page ahead of time.
2. **If it's not on the page, don't discuss it.** The page keeps the ritual focused.
3. **Use the same script every time.** Repetition turns rituals into habits.

## Rituals set the rhythm

Rituals run at different cadences, and together they keep the whole organization in sync across a quarter:

| Cadence | Ritual |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Huddle |
| Weekly | Team meeting |
| Monthly | Review |
| Quarterly | Planning |

## Rituals shape culture

A ritual repeated together becomes a habit. A habit done without thinking becomes culture: how we work here.

## Examples of rituals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Financial huddle">
    A regular review of financial metrics, budgets, and forecasts that keeps the team aligned on fiscal health.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Strategy map debate">
    A structured discussion to challenge, refine, and align on strategic priorities and direction.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Executive sync">
    A leadership alignment meeting to surface blockers, share updates, and coordinate decisions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Black hat ritual">
    A dedicated session to surface risks, concerns, and critical thinking before you commit to a plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Daily stand-up">
    A brief daily check-in to share progress, flag blockers, and align on the day's priorities.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Retrospective">
    A recurring reflection on what went well, what to improve, and what actions to take next.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
