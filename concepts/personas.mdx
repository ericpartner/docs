exIQtive has two independent ways of describing people: **personas**, which describe how someone works day to day, and **account roles**, which describe administrative access to the account. This page covers both.

## Employees with access

Employees who have access to exIQtive see the **Your Stuff** menu, which can include:

- **As Employee**
- **As a Coach**
- **As an Architect**

Which views you see depends on the badges you currently hold.

## Employee

**As Employee** is your personal readiness workspace. It shows:

- What you're responsible for
- How ready you are to perform your work
- What to prepare next
- Which Coaches can inspect you
- What badge level you currently hold

### Your readiness

At the top of the page is your **overall readiness percentage**, a real-time indicator of how prepared you are across all your responsibilities.

### Your responsibilities

Your responsibilities are listed in priority order, from highest impact on your readiness to lowest. Each one shows:

- The responsibility you're accountable for
- Your current badge level
- Your inspection progress
- A **Prepare** button to help you get ready for the next level

## Coach

A Coach holds current Coach badges and can inspect employees on those responsibilities. Coaches see the employees they're authorized to coach under **Your Stuff → As a Coach**. See [What is a Coach?](/coaching/what-is-a-coach) for the full role definition.

<Note>
A Coach can inspect only employees who don't already hold a Coach or Architect badge for the same responsibility.
</Note>

Each responsibility should ideally be supported by at least two Coaches. A common way to get there is for the Architect to also serve as a Coach and train at least one additional Coach.

## Architect

An Architect is the subject matter expert for a responsibility. Architects see the responsibilities they manage under **Your Stuff → As an Architect**. See [What is an Architect?](/coaching/what-is-an-architect) for the full role definition.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see any responsibilities?">
    You see responsibilities only after you're assigned to a position that has roles or responsibilities attached.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a new responsibility suddenly appear?">
    Your position, role, or responsibilities were updated. New responsibilities are added automatically when your position changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are my responsibilities ordered this way?">
    They're ordered by how much improving each one would increase your overall readiness. The ones at the top have the biggest impact.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does my readiness percentage mean?">
    It shows how prepared you are across all your assigned responsibilities, based on your inspection progress and badge levels.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my badge Novice?">
    Novice means you're just starting this responsibility and haven't yet completed inspections for a higher badge level.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the Prepare button do?">
    Prepare opens your learning materials, inspection checklists, and the steps required to move to your next badge level.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can inspect me?">
    Only Coaches and Architects for that responsibility can inspect you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I request an inspection yet?">
    You may need to complete preparation steps or checkpoints before inspections become available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my readiness change suddenly?">
    Readiness changes when inspections are completed, responsibilities are added or removed, or badge levels are updated.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I earn a new badge?">
    Your badge level updates, your readiness increases, and your growth path moves to the next level.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I remove a responsibility myself?">
    No. Responsibilities are assigned automatically through your position. Contact your manager or administrator if something looks wrong.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my readiness 0%?">
    Usually because your responsibilities are newly assigned and you haven't started preparation or inspections yet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account roles

The personas above describe how someone works. **Account roles** describe administrative access to the account itself. The two are independent. See [Account roles and permissions](/account/roles-and-permissions) for the full comparison.

### Owner

The Owner created the account or received ownership by transfer. Every account has exactly one Owner, and only the Owner can transfer ownership. See [What is an Account Owner?](/account/account-owner)

### Co-Owner

A Co-Owner has full administrative access, including Settings and user management, but can't transfer ownership. An account can have multiple Co-Owners. See [What is a Co-Owner?](/account/co-owner)

### Administrator

An Administrator can access and operate all day-to-day modules (Organization, Readiness, All Tasks, and more) but can't access Settings or manage account users. See [What is an Administrator?](/account/administrator)
