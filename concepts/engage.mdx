Engage is the fourth step of the exIQtive method. Employees who know their responsibilities, are ready for them, and are paid fairly still need to want to give their best. Engage helps you do three things:

1. **Fit people to the right job.**
2. **Manage each person their way.**
3. **Measure and act on engagement.**

## What drives engagement

Engagement is the discretionary effort people choose to give beyond the minimum. Four forces shape it, and each one can pull effort up or down:

- **Job:** how well the work fits the person.
- **Manager:** how the person is managed.
- **People:** the team around them.
- **Organization:** the culture of the whole company.

## Measure engagement

<iframe src="https://watchclueso.com/embed/p59grfpj6seevevq" title="How to measure engagement" style={{ width: "100%", aspectRatio: "16/9", border: "none" }} allow="fullscreen; autoplay; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen />

Engage will measure the four forces, plus overall engagement, with a short survey built on the Predictive Index category model. It will work in four steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Anonymous survey">
    Employees answer a short survey. Their answers stay anonymous.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Driver scores">
    exIQtive.com scores each driver: Job, Manager, People, and Organization.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Team reports">
    Leaders see results for each team, so they can see where engagement is strong and where it isn't.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Action plan">
    Each team turns its results into actions, so measuring leads to change.
  </Step>
</Steps>
