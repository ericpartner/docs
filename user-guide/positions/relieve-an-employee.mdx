## Relieving an Employee from an Assignment

Need to take someone off a specific assignment or position? Use the Relieve function to make the change effective from a date you choose. Here’s how to do it:

1. Navigate to the employee’s profile and select their Primary Position.
   {/* MEDIA TODO: Clueso screenshot 634c793d-de07-412f-9889-8db03b4c520d.png */}
2. Click Relieve to start the process.
3. Set the desired relieve date in the calendar popup.
   {/* MEDIA TODO: Clueso screenshot 069cb03d-0ff9-46eb-9579-a2166c6b9e06.png */}
4. Confirm the date—like selecting November 30th—and click Apply.
   {/* MEDIA TODO: Clueso screenshots cffb92de-92a7-4e88-b837-ef753baa151e.png, b4cf8632-fd43-48b9-9e2d-86cf5926c4c4.png */}
5. Review the change and click Relieve to finalize.
   {/* MEDIA TODO: Clueso screenshot c1c7468a-06f7-4802-87ac-852125d4a977.png */}

The employee’s assignment will end as of the date you picked. Use this feature when staff move roles, finish projects, or leave the organization to keep your records accurate.
