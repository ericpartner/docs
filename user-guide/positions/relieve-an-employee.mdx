---
title: "Relieve an employee from a position"
description: "End an employee's assignment to a position as of a date you choose."
---

Use **Relieve** when someone moves roles, finishes a project, or leaves the organization. The assignment ends as of the date you pick, and your records stay accurate.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Primary Position">
    Go to the employee's profile and select their **Primary Position**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Relieve">
    Click **Relieve** to start the process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the relieve date">
    Choose the date in the calendar popup, for example November 30, then click **Apply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and finish">
    Review the change, then click **Relieve** to finalize.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The employee's assignment ends as of the date you picked.
