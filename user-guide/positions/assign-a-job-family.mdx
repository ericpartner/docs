Assign a job family to a position to place it within your organizational structure. You can remove it at any time.

## Assign a job family

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the position form">
    Navigate to the **Position** form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Job Family">
    Click **Job Family**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the job family">
    Choose the job family from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the level">
    Choose the **Job Family Level**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign">
    Click **Assign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a job family

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the remove icon">
    Click the remove icon next to the job family.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Remove**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a New Job Family in Your Organization

<iframe src="https://watchclueso.com/embed/ltffbmlnw58zuu1i" title="Adding a new job family to your organization" style={{ width: "100%", aspectRatio: "16/9", border: "none" }} allow="fullscreen; autoplay; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen />

Adding job families helps keep your organization’s roles structured and easy to manage. Here’s how to quickly create a new job family from the Organization page.

1. On the Organization page, click Add Job Family to open the creation dialog.
   {/* MEDIA TODO: Clueso screenshot 220ea8a8-74ad-408a-81bd-d8dfa356f26d.png */}
2. Type your desired job family name into the Name field. For example, enter “Accountant”.
   {/* MEDIA TODO: Clueso screenshot 27eb5d43-ed7c-4498-b330-afdab2b54f24.png */}
3. Click Save to add the new job family and close the dialog.
   {/* MEDIA TODO: Clueso screenshot ce651520-da11-4c30-b1b5-aa70bca0456e.png */}
4. Confirm your new job family appears in the list. It’ll be highlighted in green to show it was added successfully.
   {/* MEDIA TODO: Clueso screenshot fc56531f-375f-4911-8150-0e50de34a038.png */}

That’s it! Your new job family is now set up and ready to use. This keeps your organization list organized and makes assigning roles a breeze.
