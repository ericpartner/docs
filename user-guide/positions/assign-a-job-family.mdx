Assign a job family to a position to place it within your organizational structure. You can remove it at any time.

## Assign a job family

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the position form">
    Navigate to the **Position** form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Job Family">
    Click **Job Family**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the job family">
    Choose the job family from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the level">
    Choose the **Job Family Level**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign">
    Click **Assign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a job family

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the remove icon">
    Click the remove icon next to the job family.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Remove**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Related

Need a job family that doesn't exist yet? Add it from the **Organization** page with **Add Job Family**, enter a name, and click **Save**. The new job family appears in the list highlighted in green.
