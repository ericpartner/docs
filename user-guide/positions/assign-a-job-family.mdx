## Assign a job family

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the position form">
    Navigate to the **Position** form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Job Family">
    Click **Job Family**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the job family">
    Choose the job family from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the level">
    Choose the **Job Family Level**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign">
    Click **Assign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a job family

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the remove icon">
    Click the remove icon next to the job family.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Remove**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a New Job Family in Your Organization

<iframe src="https://watchclueso.com/embed/ltffbmlnw58zuu1i" title="Adding a new job family to your organization" style={{ width: "100%", aspectRatio: "16/9", border: "none" }} allow="fullscreen; autoplay; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen />

Adding job families helps keep your organization’s positions structured and easy to manage. Here’s how to quickly create a new job family from the Job Families page.

1. In **Fair Base Pay → Job Families**, click Add Job Family to open the creation dialog.
2. Type your desired job family name into the Name field. For example, enter “Accountant”.
3. Click Save to add the new job family and close the dialog.
4. Confirm your new job family appears in the list. It’ll be highlighted in green to show it was added successfully.

That’s it! Your new job family is now set up and ready to use. This keeps your organization list organized and makes assigning job families a breeze.
