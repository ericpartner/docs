## Set up your organization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Learn the building blocks">
    Read [Key organizational concepts](/concepts/organization) to understand how Positions, Roles and Responsibilities fit together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your structure">
    Create [Positions](/organization/positions), attach [Roles](/organization/roles) or responsibilities, and group them into [Departments](/organization/departments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your people">
    Add [Employees](/organization/employees) and [assign them to Positions](/organization/employee-position-assignments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the work">
    Set up [Responsibilities](/responsibilities/managing-responsibilities) and their [Checkpoints](/checkpoints/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give people access">
    Invite [Account Users](/account/account-users) and check [who can do what](/account/roles-and-permissions).
  </Step>
</Steps>
