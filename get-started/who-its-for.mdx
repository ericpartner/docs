exIQtive is for growing organizations starting to feel the friction of scale: strategy is clear at the top but inconsistent at the front line, people are busy but not always on the right things, and no one can confidently answer "is everyone ready?" If that sounds familiar, you're in the right place.

## Signs exIQtive is right for you

- You're moving from founder-led execution to managers and teams running things independently, and you need consistency without micromanagement
- Your organization is growing faster than your ability to make sure everyone knows what's expected of them
- You have training programs but no reliable way to know whether people can actually perform afterward
- Compensation decisions feel political or subjective, and you want a fair, transparent basis for them
- You want to identify future leaders based on demonstrated competence, not on who's most visible

## Who uses exIQtive

### Organizational Readiness Managers (ORMs)

If you're responsible for making sure your organization is ready to execute, whether that's your title or just your role in practice, exIQtive lets you define what "ready" means for every position, measure it consistently, and act on it. You see readiness across the whole organization, find the gaps, and track progress over time.

### Managers

exIQtive helps you move from managing by intuition to managing by evidence. You see what each person on your team is responsible for, their current readiness for each responsibility, and what they need to do to progress. No more guessing who's ready for more.

### Coaches and Architects

If you're a subject matter expert, exIQtive gives you a structured way to share that expertise. As an **Architect**, you create the knowledge base and standards for your responsibilities. As a **Coach**, you use those standards to inspect and certify other employees. Both roles multiply your impact beyond what you can do alone.

### Employees

exIQtive gives you complete clarity: what you're responsible for, what the standard looks like, how you're evaluated, and what your next step is. You earn badges that objectively certify your competence, with no politics and no ambiguity. Your growth path is visible and yours to drive.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to be a large company to use exIQtive?">
    No. exIQtive works best when founder-led execution is starting to strain, typically somewhere between 20 and 500 employees, though the principles apply beyond that. What matters is whether consistency, clarity, and scalable performance matter to your organization right now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="We already have performance reviews. How is this different?">
    Performance reviews measure what happened. exIQtive measures whether someone is ready to perform, before problems show up. It's proactive rather than retrospective, and based on demonstrated capability against a defined standard rather than a manager's annual impression.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is exIQtive only for one function, or the whole company?">
    exIQtive covers your entire workforce: every position, role, and responsibility across the organization. It's not a tool for one department; it's how your whole company defines, measures, and develops readiness.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
