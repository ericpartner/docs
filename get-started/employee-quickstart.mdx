Welcome to exIQtive.com. You've been assigned to a position, and with it a set of responsibilities. For each responsibility you earn a badge that shows how ready you are, and your badges add up to your Readiness. This page takes you through your first day. New here? Start with [Welcome to exIQtive.com](/employees/welcome) for the ideas behind it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept your invitation">
    Open the invitation email, create your password on the Join page, and select **Create account**. You're signed in straight away. See [Join and sign in](/employees/join-and-sign-in).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go through the welcome wizard">
    Confirm your name, add a photo if you like, and see the four badge levels you can earn. It takes a minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your responsibilities">
    The wizard ends on **Your Stuff → As Employee → Your Responsibilities**. Every responsibility you hold is listed there, most important first. See [Your Stuff](/employees/your-stuff).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare on one responsibility">
    Select **Prepare** on a responsibility. Work through its checkpoints: watch the video or read the article, then take the quiz. See [Prepare on a responsibility](/employees/prepare-on-a-responsibility).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request an inspection">
    When you feel ready, select **Request Inspection**, choose a Coach, and send the request. Follow it on **Your Inspection Requests**. See [Request an inspection](/employees/request-an-inspection).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn your badge">
    When the Coach passes you, you earn the badge for that level and your Readiness goes up. See [The Badge Ladder](/user-guide/readiness/badge-ladder).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep going

- Check **Your Tasks** for anything assigned to you, such as a video to upload. See [Tasks](/readiness/tasks).
- Ask **IQ** when you're not sure how something works. See [Ask IQ](/iq/ask-iq).
- Use exIQtive.com on your phone too. See [Mobile apps and phone browser](/get-started/mobile).
