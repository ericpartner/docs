As your company grows, the link between what you're trying to achieve and what your people do every day gets weaker. exIQtive is a methodology that transforms business strategy into aligned behavior at scale. exIQtive.com is the software that puts the methodology to work in your organization.

## The problem exIQtive solves

From a small team where the founder touches everything, to 50, to 500 people, something breaks. You hire more people and add more managers, but the knowledge of who does what, how it's done, and what good looks like stays in a few heads. The strategy that made sense in the leadership meeting never quite reaches the front line.

Five problems follow, and each step of the method solves one:

| Problem | What it looks like | The step that solves it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nobody is sure who owns what | Job descriptions are vague, responsibilities overlap or fall through the cracks, and procedures live as tribal knowledge. | [Clarify](/concepts/clarify) |
| Readiness is assumed, not proven | People take on work they were never verified to do. Training is counted as competence, and there's no visible path to the next position. | [Certify](/concepts/certify) |
| Pay feels arbitrary | Salaries drift with negotiation and history instead of the value of the job. That erodes trust and creates legal exposure as pay transparency laws spread. | [Pay fairly](/concepts/pay-fairly) |
| Disengagement stays invisible | You find out someone was in the wrong job, or managed the wrong way, when they quit. | [Engage](/concepts/engage) |
| Execution drifts | Without a regular rhythm, goals, responsibilities and readiness go out of date, and the same problems keep coming back. | [Synchronize](/concepts/synchronize) |

exIQtive is built to fix all five, so your company can grow without depending on its founders' memory.

## What exIQtive does

exIQtive turns strategy into behavior in five steps: **Clarify**, **Certify**, **Pay fairly**, **Engage** and **Synchronize**. Each step answers a question every growing organization struggles with, from "what does each employee need to do?" to "are your people ready?" See [The exIQtive method](/concepts/method) for how the steps fit together.

## Readiness

Readiness is the heart of the Certify step. Before you can scale anything, you need to know whether your people can perform their responsibilities at the required standard, not just whether they were hired and trained. exIQtive makes Readiness visible and measurable: not a gut feeling, but a real percentage backed by structured inspections and certifications.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is exIQtive an HR system?">
    No. exIQtive is a methodology for aligning behavior with strategy, and exIQtive.com is the software that applies it. It doesn't manage payroll, benefits, contracts, or employment administration. It focuses on whether your people know what's expected, are genuinely ready to perform it, and are paid fairly for it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is exIQtive different from a learning management system?">
    A learning management system tracks whether people completed training. exIQtive.com tracks whether they can actually do the work: independently, consistently, and to the standard you need. Completion and capability are not the same thing, and exIQtive.com is built around capability.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does exIQtive work for small companies, or only large ones?">
    exIQtive is designed for the growth journey, from the point where founder-led execution starts to break down, upward. If consistency, clarity, and scalable performance matter to you, exIQtive is relevant regardless of your exact headcount.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
