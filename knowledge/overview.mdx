Knowledge holds what your organization knows about doing the work: short videos, articles and quizzes. It's part of the [Certify](/concepts/certify) step. In Certify, an Architect first **captures knowledge**, then turns it into [checkpoints](/checkpoints/overview) that Coaches inspect against.

## Videos, articles and quizzes on checkpoints

Each checkpoint can carry the knowledge an employee needs to prepare:

- A short **video** that shows how the work is done.
- An **article** with the steps written out. You can write it yourself, or import it from [Scribe](/checkpoints/importing-a-scribe-article) or a [PDF](/checkpoints/importing-a-pdf). See [Editing an article](/checkpoints/editing-an-article).
- A **quiz** that checks the employee understood it. See [Quizzes](/checkpoints/quizzes).

Employees study the video or article and take the quiz before they're inspected.

## What it takes to pass a checkpoint

The checkpoint decides what it takes to pass:

| To pass requires | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Quiz only** | The employee passes the quiz. |
| **Quiz and Inspection** | The employee passes the quiz, then a Coach inspects them. |
| **Inspection only** | A Coach inspects the employee. No quiz is needed. |

The inspection protocol belongs to the checkpoint. See [Inspecting a responsibility](/coaching/inspecting-a-responsibility).
