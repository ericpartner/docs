**View As** lets you see exIQtive from an employee's point of view without changing anything. Use it to check that an employee has the right access and to troubleshoot what they report.

## Who can use View As

Any administrator with access to **Organization > Employees** can use View As.

## View as an employee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start View As">
    Select the **View As** option for the employee you want to see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse as the employee">
    exIQtive now looks the way it does for that employee. Browse every screen available to them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore each section">
    Navigate around to check every section the employee can reach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exit View As">
    When you're done, exit **View As** to return to your administrator view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can't do in View As

View As is view-only. While it's on, you can't create, edit, or commit to anything. Nothing you do changes the employee's data or yours.
