The Jobs settings control two things: whether [job families](/fair-pay/job-families) are turned on, and how many value bands your [job categories](/fair-pay/job-categories) are grouped into.

Go to **Settings → Configuration → Jobs**.

<Info>
  Only Owners and Co-Owners can change these settings. Administrators can see each job category's score and band, but can't change the number of bands.
</Info>

## Turn job families on or off

Job families are off by default. Turn them on here when you want to group positions by discipline and seniority.

Turning job families off hides them, but keeps all your families, levels, and position assignments. They reappear when you turn job families back on.

## What value bands are

Every job category has a Total Score. Value bands split the range of possible scores into groups, so you can compare job categories of similar value.

A band has no name or number. It is identified only by its range of points.

The bands are near-equal in size, not exactly equal. The range of scores rarely divides evenly, so band sizes can differ by one point.

## Change the number of bands

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Jobs">
    Go to **Settings → Configuration** and select the **Jobs** tile.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the number of bands">
    Type a whole number from 2 to 10 in **Number of bands**. New accounts start with 5.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the preview">
    The preview shows the point range of each band as you type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. To undo your change, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Save** and **Cancel** appear only while your entry differs from the saved number. If the entry isn't a whole number from 2 to 10, **Save** stays disabled until it is valid.

The change applies right away. You don't need to recalculate anything, and every job category moves to its new band automatically.

## How the score is built

The weighting of the four scoring factors is fixed, so you can't change it. It follows the published EIGE scoring scheme, so it isn't tuned to any one result.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I name the bands?">
    No. A band is identified by its point range only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change the weighting of the scoring factors?">
    No. The weighting is fixed for every account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to existing job categories when I change the number of bands?">
    Nothing is lost. Each job category keeps its score, and its band updates to match the new setting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
