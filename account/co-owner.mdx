---
title: "What is a Co-Owner?"
description: "A Co-Owner has full admin access, including Settings and user management, but can't transfer ownership or close the account."
---

A **Co-Owner** is one of the four Account Roles in exIQtive, just below Owner and above Administrator and App User. A Co-Owner has full administrative access to the account, including Settings and user management. Unlike Owner, an account can have several Co-Owners at once.

## What a Co-Owner can do

What sets a Co-Owner apart from an Administrator is Settings and user management. A Co-Owner can:

- Access Settings and manage account configuration
- Give access to new users
- Suspend or restore access
- Change email addresses
- Add or remove other Co-Owners and Administrators

<Warning>
A Co-Owner can suspend or restore Administrators and App Users, but can't suspend another Co-Owner. A Co-Owner can change their own email address, but not another Co-Owner's.
</Warning>

## What a Co-Owner can't do

Two actions are reserved for the Owner: **transferring ownership** and **closing the account**.

## Co-Owner vs Personas

Account Roles are separate from Personas, so a Co-Owner doesn't need to be an Employee at all. An external consultant can administer the account as a Co-Owner without holding any Position in the organization.
