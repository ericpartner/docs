Use Branding to save your company logo with your account.

Go to **Settings → Configuration → Branding**.

<Info>
  Only Owners and Co-Owners can manage the logo. Administrators can't see Settings.
</Info>

## Upload a logo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Branding">
    Go to **Settings → Configuration** and select the **Branding** tile.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your file">
    Click **Upload logo**, or drag and drop your file onto the upload area.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the preview">
    Your logo appears as soon as the upload finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### File requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | Transparent PNG or SVG (preferred), or JPG or WebP. |
| **Size** | Up to 2 MB. |

If your file is the wrong format or too large, you see a message under the upload area and nothing is uploaded. If an upload fails, try again. Your current logo stays as it was.

## Replace your logo

With a logo already saved, click **Replace** and choose a new file. The same requirements apply. The new logo takes the place of the old one.

## Remove your logo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Remove">
    A **Remove logo?** confirmation appears.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Remove logo**. You can upload it again anytime.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Settings?">
    Settings is available to Owners and Co-Owners only. Ask your Owner or a Co-Owner to upload the logo, or to change your permission level. See [permission levels](/account/users-and-permissions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which file works best?">
    A transparent PNG or an SVG. Both keep the edges clean on any background.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
