This page answers the two questions administrators ask most about where their data lives.

## Where exIQtive.com runs

exIQtive.com is cloud-based software. You use it in a web browser, and there is nothing to install. It runs on cloud servers hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). exIQtive hosts and maintains the platform and its storage, so your team doesn't need to run any servers.

## Who owns your data

You own the data in your account. exIQtive hosts it and maintains the platform and storage it lives on.
