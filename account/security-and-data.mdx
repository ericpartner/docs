This page answers the questions administrators ask most about where their data lives and how AI uses it.

## Where exIQtive.com runs

exIQtive.com is cloud-based software. You use it in a web browser, and there is nothing to install. It runs on cloud servers hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). We host and maintain the platform and its storage, so your team doesn't need to run any servers.

## Who owns your data

You own the data in your account. We host it and maintain the platform and storage it lives on.

## How we use AI and your data

IQ, the AI capability in exIQtive.com, uses Google's Gemini models for its AI features. See [IQ](/iq/overview) for what IQ does.

We don't train any AI model on your data. We use the Gemini API under Google's paid terms. Under those terms, Google states that it doesn't use your prompts or the responses to improve its products, and that it processes them under its Data Processing Addendum. Google also states that it logs prompts and responses for a limited period, solely to detect and prevent violations of its Prohibited Use Policy.

IQ suggests and people decide. For how IQ is designed and where it sits under the EU AI Act, see [EU AI Act position](/iq/eu-ai-act-position).

### Google's disclosures

These are Google's own statements about how it handles data sent to the Gemini API:

- [Gemini API Additional Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) explains how Google treats prompts and responses for paid services.
- [Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform and zero data retention](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/data-governance) describes Google's data governance for its enterprise AI platform, including its commitment not to train or fine-tune models on customer data without permission.
- [AI trust and transparency](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/ai-trust-and-transparency) covers the training restriction in the Google Cloud Service Specific Terms.
