The **Organizational Readiness Manager (ORM)** owns exIQtive's readiness system for your organization. The ORM makes sure work expectations are clearly defined, covered by trained people, and continuously measured, so readiness is visible, measurable, and actively improved rather than assumed.

## What the ORM does

- Maintains the living catalog of roles and responsibilities
- Makes sure every position is properly covered
- Oversees the inspection checklists, videos, and quizzes used to certify readiness
- Trains and certifies Architects
- Makes sure each responsibility has enough trained Coaches to keep the coaching pipeline running
- Removes badges or retires responsibilities when needed

## How the ORM differs from Coach and Architect

Coach and Architect are badges held per Responsibility. ORM is an organization-wide role. The ORM designates Architects directly, rather than that status being earned through inspection.
