---
title: "What is an Account Owner?"
description: "The Account Owner is the top Account Role: one per account, with full admin access plus ownership transfer and account closure."
---

The **Account Owner** is the highest of the four Account Roles in exIQtive, above Co-Owner, Administrator, and App User. Every account has exactly one Owner at a time: the person who created the account or received ownership through a transfer.

## What the Owner can do

The Owner has everything a Co-Owner has (full access to Settings, account configuration, and user management) plus two things no one else can do:

- **Transfer ownership** to another account user
- **Close the account**

<Note>
The Owner can't be suspended. To remove the current Owner's access, ownership has to be transferred to someone else first.
</Note>

## Owner vs Personas

Account Roles are separate from Personas, so being the Owner says nothing about someone's daily work in the organization. An Owner is usually also an Employee and may hold Coach, Architect, or ORM personas, but nothing requires it. An external consultant with no Position in the organization can administer the account as a Co-Owner.
