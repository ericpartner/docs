---
title: "What is a Coach?"
description: "A Coach is appointed by an Architect to inspect other employees on a responsibility and award or give Apprentice and Proficient badges."
---

A **Coach** is a Proficient badge holder who has been trained and appointed by an Architect to inspect other employees on the same responsibility. The Coach badge sits at 125% Readiness: above Novice, Apprentice, and Proficient, and below Architect.

## What a Coach does

Once appointed, a Coach can conduct inspections and award or give **Apprentice** and **Proficient** badges to others. They evaluate each employee's knowledge, abilities, and behavior (KAB) against the standards the Architect has defined in the inspection protocol.

## Who can act as a Coach

An employee with a current **Coach** badge on a responsibility can coach on it. An **Architect** on the responsibility can also act as a Coach and conduct inspections directly. See [What is an Architect?](/coaching/what-is-an-architect).

## Coach is per responsibility

Badges are held per responsibility, not per person. The same employee can be a Coach for one responsibility while holding a lower badge, like Proficient or Apprentice, for others.
