## Start a chat

When you open IQ with an empty chat, you see a greeting with your first name, for example "Hi Eric, how can I help?". Below it, a short line tells you that IQ answers questions about the user guide and your account data, from what you can see, and cites its sources.

Under the greeting you see up to four suggested questions, for example "Who is falling behind on readiness?". Select one to send it as your first message. The greeting and suggestions disappear once the conversation starts.

Suggestions only include questions IQ can answer for you. If you can't see employee readiness, you won't get readiness questions. If the suggestions can't load, you still see the greeting and can type your own question.

## Start a new chat

Select **\+ New chat** in the header of the side panel or the full-screen view. IQ starts an empty conversation with the same opening. Your previous conversation stays in your history.

## Choose how IQ is displayed

Select **View** in the header to choose one of three modes. The current mode has a check mark.

| Mode | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Sidebar** | IQ docks to the right side of the page. This is the default. |
| **Floating** | IQ appears as a card in the bottom-right corner over the page. The left navigation and page content don't move, and you can keep clicking the page behind it. |
| **Fullscreen** | IQ opens as a full page with your earlier chats listed on the side. |

Your conversation stays the same when you switch modes. If IQ is writing an answer, it keeps writing in the new mode. Switching modes doesn't use any credits.

IQ remembers your choice for your account in the browser you're using. The next time you open IQ, it opens in the mode you last chose.

To leave fullscreen, select **Floating** or **Sidebar** in the **View** menu, select the close button, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>. IQ returns to the mode you were in before, on the conversation you last had open.

In every mode, the close button is the first control on the left of the header. **\+ New chat** and **View** are on the right.
