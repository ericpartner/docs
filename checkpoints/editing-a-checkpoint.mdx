This page walks through the checkpoint editor itself: what changes depending on how a checkpoint starts, and how each tab works. For checkpoint statuses and other operations (Retire, Archive, Move, bulk actions), see [Checkpoints](/checkpoints/overview). For the article writing tools, see [Editing an article](/checkpoints/editing-an-article).

## Landing on the checkpoint editor

You reach the same editor two ways, depending on whether the checkpoint already exists:

- **New checkpoint**: creating a checkpoint lands you on it in Draft, with a single editing surface. Nothing is visible to employees until you click **Activate**.
- **Existing Active checkpoint**: open it and click **Propose Changes** to get a two-tab view, **Active Checkpoint** and **Proposed Changes**. Make your edits on the Proposed Changes side; employees keep seeing the current Active version until you click **Publish**. Use **Copy content from the active Checkpoint** to start from what's already live instead of from scratch.

Click **Save** to save as you go.

<Warning>
Changes aren't saved automatically. Save periodically if you're editing over a longer session.
</Warning>

Only one person can hold the edit lock at a time. If someone else is already editing, you'll see who (with a live avatar) and can ask to take over.

## Choosing how the checkpoint starts

When you create a checkpoint, you pick one of four starting points:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Start with a Video" icon="video">
    Upload a video of up to 5 minutes. The editor opens once it finishes processing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Start with an Article" icon="file-lines">
    Write the microlearning directly in exIQtive's built-in editor. See [Editing an article](/checkpoints/editing-an-article) for the writing and formatting tools.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No learning content needed" icon="clipboard-check">
    Skip microlearning and go straight to the inspection criteria. You can add microlearning later from within the checkpoint. This option includes a note field for prep instructions to the employee.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Task an employee to upload a video" icon="user-plus">
    Assign the video upload to an employee instead of doing it yourself. The checkpoint waits in a pending-upload state, with reminders, until they deliver it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The three tabs: Video/Article, Quiz, Inspection

Inside the editor, content is organized into up to three tabs. A mode setting on the checkpoint controls which tabs apply: **Both** (quiz and inspection), **Skip inspection**, or **Skip quiz**.

- **Video/Article**: the microlearning itself. For a video, this is also where you generate and manage an AI-suggested breakdown (chapters or steps) from the footage once it's ready.
- **Quiz**: the multiple-choice questions an employee answers after completing the microlearning. See [Quizzes for checkpoints](/checkpoints/quizzes).
- **Inspection**: the criteria a Coach checks off while observing the employee do the work.

Each tab shows a validity indicator, so you can see at a glance whether it has what it needs before you activate or publish.

## Version history

Every time you activate or publish, exIQtive creates a new version. Open the version history to see past versions, the publish note for each, and an automatically generated summary of what changed.

## Activate vs. publish

| Action | When you use it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Activate** | The first time, on a brand-new Draft | Creates version 1 and makes the checkpoint live |
| **Publish** | On the Proposed Changes side of an existing checkpoint | Adds the next version; employees see the update with no interruption |

## Discarding your edits

To throw away what you've started, use **Discard Draft** (or the equivalent on Proposed Changes):

- For a checkpoint that was never activated, this deletes it outright.
- For proposed changes to an Active checkpoint, it drops your edits and leaves the live version untouched.
