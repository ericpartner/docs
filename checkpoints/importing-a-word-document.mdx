Bring an existing Word document, such as a procedure or training handout, into a checkpoint's article. exIQtive converts the document's headings, text, lists, tables and images into editable article content, so you don't have to retype it.

## Import a Word document into an article

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the checkpoint">
    Open the checkpoint you want to work on and click **Edit**, or **Lock to Edit** if the checkpoint is already Active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Article tab">
    In the Microlearning section, select the **Article** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Import from Word">
    Open the **⋯** menu next to the Article, Quiz and Inspection tabs and select **Import from Word**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your document">
    Choose a `.docx` file from your computer, or drag it into the window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the import to finish">
    The window closes and the converted content appears in the editor. A short summary tells you what was imported and what wasn't carried over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Check the content, make any changes, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What carries over

| In your document | In the article |
| --- | --- |
| Title and Heading styles | Headings. Levels deeper than the editor supports use the deepest level it has. |
| Paragraphs | Paragraphs. Empty paragraphs used only for spacing are dropped. |
| Bulleted and numbered lists | Bullet and numbered lists, including nested lists |
| Tables | Tables |
| Bold, italic, underline and links | The same formatting and links |
| PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP pictures | Images, placed where they appear in the document, with their alt text |
| Tracked changes | Accepted text: insertions are kept and deletions are dropped |

exIQtive leaves out headers, footers and page numbers. Table of contents entries stay as text.

## What doesn't carry over

- **Comments, footnotes and endnotes.**
- **Text boxes, charts, SmartArt and drawings.** Add them to the article yourself.
- **Pictures in other formats**, such as EMF and WMF.
- **Merged table cells.** exIQtive splits them and keeps the content in the first cell.
- **Page layout and document styling.**

The import summary tells you what was left out, so you know what to check.

## Before you import

- Use a `.docx` file. If you have an older `.doc` file, open it in Word and save it as `.docx` first.
- Macro-enabled documents (`.docm`) and password-protected documents can't be imported. Remove the password in Word, then try again.
- The imported content appears as an unsaved draft. Click **Save** to keep it. See [Editing an article](/checkpoints/editing-an-article).
- If the article already has content, importing replaces it. exIQtive asks you to confirm first. You can't add imported content to existing text.
- If the import fails, nothing is added to the article and exIQtive tells you why.
- Importing the same document twice gives you the same result.
