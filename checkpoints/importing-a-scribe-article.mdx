Create a new checkpoint for your responsibility by importing detailed steps and images directly from a Scribe knowledge article. This keeps all your process documentation in one place and ensures teams have the information they need.

## Add a checkpoint and import a Scribe article

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a checkpoint">
    Click **Add Checkpoint** in your responsibility section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the checkpoint">
    Name your checkpoint, for example, "can correctly link retainer payments." Set the type to **Knowledge Article**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Import from Scribe">
    Select **Import from Scribe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your Scribe link">
    Copy the Scribe article link. Paste the link into the import field and start the import.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the import to finish">
    Wait for the import to finish. The Scribe content, complete with images, appears in your checkpoint.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You now have a checkpoint populated with step-by-step instructions and screenshots from Scribe. This keeps your processes clear, visually rich, and easy for your team to follow.

## Before you import

- The Scribe link must be a public share link. If the guide is private, make it public in Scribe, then try again.
- The imported content appears as an unsaved draft. Click **Save** to keep it. See [Editing an article](/checkpoints/editing-an-article).
- If the article already has content, importing replaces it. exIQtive asks you to confirm first.
