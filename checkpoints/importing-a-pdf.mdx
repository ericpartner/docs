Bring an existing PDF, such as a procedure, manual or training handout, into a checkpoint's article. exIQtive converts the PDF's text, headings, lists, tables and images into editable article content, so you don't have to retype it.

<Note>
  Copying text from a PDF viewer and pasting it into the article editor gives you plain, unformatted text with no images. Import the file instead to keep the structure.
</Note>

## Import a PDF into an article

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the checkpoint">
    Open the checkpoint you want to work on and click **Edit**, or **Lock to Edit** if the checkpoint is already Active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Article tab">
    In the Microlearning section, select the **Article** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Import from PDF">
    Open the **⋯** menu next to the Article, Quiz and Inspection tabs and select **Import from PDF**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your PDF">
    Choose a PDF from your computer, or drag it into the window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the import to finish">
    The window closes and the converted content appears in the editor. A short summary tells you what was imported and what wasn't carried over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Check the content, make any changes, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What carries over

| In your PDF | In the article |
| --- | --- |
| Headings | Headings |
| Paragraphs | Paragraphs, with lines that wrapped on the page joined back together |
| Bulleted and numbered lists | Bullet and numbered lists, including nested lists |
| Tables | Tables |
| Bold, italic, underline and links | The same formatting and links |
| Pictures | Images, placed where they appear in the PDF |

exIQtive leaves out repeated page headers and footers, page numbers, and the dotted lines and page numbers in a table of contents. Table of contents entries stay as text.

## What doesn't carry over

- **Charts and drawings.** Only embedded pictures are imported. Add charts and drawings to the article yourself.
- **Scanned PDFs.** The PDF needs selectable text. A PDF made from scanned pages can't be imported.
- **Password-protected PDFs.** Remove the password in your PDF tool, then try again.
- **Page layout and visual styling**, form fields and annotations.

Some tables and images may not convert cleanly. The import summary tells you when that happens, so you know what to check.

## Before you import

- The imported content appears as an unsaved draft. Click **Save** to keep it. See [Editing an article](/checkpoints/editing-an-article).
- If the article already has content, importing replaces it. exIQtive asks you to confirm first. You can't add imported content to existing text.
- If the import fails, nothing is added to the article and exIQtive tells you why.
- Importing the same PDF twice gives you the same result.
