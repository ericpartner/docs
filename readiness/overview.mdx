description: "What Readiness measures in exIQtive and where to see it for yourself, a department, or a position."
---

Readiness is exIQtive's measure of whether an employee can perform their Responsibilities independently, consistently, and to the required standard. This section covers how Readiness works, how it's calculated, and where to see it.

## Where to see readiness

- **Your own Readiness** — your Path to Readiness view shows your Responsibilities and badges.
- **A department's Readiness** — go to **Account → Organization → Departments**. Every row shows a Readiness bar and percentage, rolled up across that Department and its sub-departments. See [View department readiness](/organization/departments#view-department-readiness).
- **A position's Readiness** — see [Positions](/organization/positions).
