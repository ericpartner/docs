A **Role** is a collection of Responsibilities. A Position has Roles attached to it, and each Role has Responsibilities attached to it. When an employee is assigned to a Position, they automatically inherit every Responsibility through that Position's Roles.

For the underlying definitions, see [Key organizational concepts](/concepts/organization).

## How roles work

The exIQtive structure is:

**Employee → Position → Role → Responsibility → Readiness**

A Role is the grouping mechanism. Instead of attaching Responsibilities one by one to every Position, you define a Role once, with its set of Responsibilities, and attach it to as many Positions as need it. When the Role changes, every Position that uses it picks up the change automatically.

## Where to find roles

Go to **Account → Organization → Roles**. The list shows every Role in your account, with its status, the number of attached Responsibilities, and the Positions it's attached to.

## Role lifecycle

**Draft → Active → Retired → Archived**

| Status | What it means | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Newly created and not yet in use. Doesn't distribute Responsibilities to employees. | Rename, Attach Responsibilities, Delete, Activate |
| **Active** | Live. Can be attached to Positions and distributes its Responsibilities to assigned employees. | Rename, Attach or detach Responsibilities, Retire |
| **Retired** | Removed from active use and detached from all Positions. Responsibilities no longer flow through it. Data is preserved. | Rename, Reactivate, Archive |
| **Archived** | Stored for historical reference only. | Unarchive (returns the Role to Retired) |

## Add a new role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Roles">
    Go to **Account → Organization → Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the role">
    Click **+ Add Role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the role">
    Enter a **Name**. It's required, 3 to 120 characters, with a live character counter, and it must be unique in your account. If the name is already used, you see "This name is already in use. Enter a different name." under the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add context (optional)">
    Add a **Context** of up to 500 characters, or click **Suggest** to have IQ draft it. You can write a longer Context later on the Role's Context tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save as Draft**. The button is enabled once the name is valid and unique and IQ isn't writing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Role is created in **Draft**. You can edit or delete it, but it won't distribute Responsibilities to employees until you activate it.

### Suggest a context with IQ

**Suggest** is available once the name is 3 to 120 characters. IQ drafts the Context from the name and your account context. While IQ writes, the button becomes **Stop**, which keeps what was written so far. If the Context already has text, you're asked **Replace existing context?** before IQ overwrites it. After a suggestion, the button reads **Re-suggest**.

### Create and attach from a picker

When you attach a Role to a Position, the picker's **\+ Create and attach new** row opens the same dialog, with your typed name filled in and a read-only "Attaching to…" line. The button reads **Save as Draft and Attach**, so the new Draft Role is created and attached in one step.

## Role actions

All actions are in the three-dot menu (⋯) on the right side of the Role row.

| Action | Available when | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Activate** | Draft | Moves the Role to Active so it can be attached to Positions and distribute its Responsibilities. Can't return to Draft. |
| **Rename** | Always | Updates the Role name everywhere. No assignments are affected. The dialog uses the same layout as Add Role and its button reads **Save**. On an Archived Role you can rename it, but Context is read-only. |
| **Retire** | Active | Detaches the Role from all Positions and removes it from active use. Employees who inherited Responsibilities through it lose them. |
| **Reactivate** | Retired | Returns the Role to Active so it can be attached to Positions again. |
| **Archive** | Retired | Moves the Role into long-term storage. |
| **Unarchive** | Archived | Returns the Role to Retired. From there you can reactivate it. |
| **Delete** | Draft only | Permanently deletes the Role. This can't be undone. |

## Attach responsibilities to a role

A Role needs at least one active Responsibility before it meaningfully contributes to employee Readiness.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the role">
    Find the Role in the Roles list and click its name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a responsibility">
    Click **+ Attach Responsibility**, then search for and select the Responsibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm. Any employee assigned to a Position that includes this Role automatically receives the Responsibility.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To detach a Responsibility, open the Role, find the Responsibility in the list, and use the detach action in its row menu.

## Suggest responsibilities with IQ

On a Role, **Suggest with AI** opens an IQ side panel that proposes 10 to 20 new Responsibilities for that Role. IQ uses the Role's name and purpose and your account's business context. It leaves out Responsibilities already on the Role and already in your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Open the Role and choose **Suggest with AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for suggestions">
    IQ shows its thinking while it prepares suggestions on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and attach">
    For each suggestion you want, click **Create and Attach**. The Responsibility is created and attached to the Role right away. Suggestions you haven't actioned are listed with a counter, for example "2 of 20".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine (optional)">
    Type a refinement, such as "focus on industrial manufacturing". Refining discards the remaining suggestions and generates a new set. Anything you already created stays attached and isn't suggested again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The chat box is disabled while IQ is generating. A transcript on the left records what was suggested and what you created.

If you close the panel with suggestions left, you're asked **Exit before finishing?** Closing discards the remaining suggestions and the conversation, but never removes anything you already created. Each time you open the panel it starts a fresh session.

## Attach a role to a position

You attach Roles to Positions from the Position detail view, not from the Roles list. See [Positions](/organization/positions) for the steps.

## Role validity

A Role is **valid** when it's Active and has at least one active Responsibility attached. A Role is **invalid** when it's Active but has no active Responsibilities. An invalid Role attached to a Position makes that Position invalid, which blocks employee assignment.

See [Key organizational concepts](/concepts/organization) for the full Position validity rules.

## Rules and constraints

- A Role must be **Active** to distribute Responsibilities to employees.
- A Role can be attached to any number of Positions.
- A Responsibility can be attached to any number of Roles.
- Deleting a Role is permanent and only possible in Draft.
- Retiring a Role detaches it from all Positions, and employees lose the associated Responsibilities.
- An Active Role with no active Responsibilities is invalid.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't employees see the Responsibilities I attached to a Role?">
    Check that: (1) the Role is Active, (2) the Role is attached to a Position, (3) the Position is Active, (4) an employee is assigned to the Position, and (5) the Responsibilities themselves are Active.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I attach the same Role to multiple Positions?">
    Yes. Roles are designed to be reused. A single Role like "Customer Support" can be attached to both a Team Leader Position and a Support Agent Position.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to employees when I retire a Role?">
    They lose the Responsibilities that came through that Role, and their Readiness for those Responsibilities drops to 0% (Novice). Their badge history is preserved. If the Responsibilities are attached to the same Position again through another Role, they return.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between retiring a Role and detaching it from a Position?">
    Detaching removes the Role from one specific Position. Retiring removes it from all Positions at once and takes it out of active use entirely.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I rename a Role without affecting assignments?">
    Yes. Renaming updates the name everywhere but doesn't change any assignments or affect employee Readiness.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
