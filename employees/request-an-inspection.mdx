You earn a badge when a Coach inspects you and you meet the standard. You start the inspection yourself, when you feel ready. Nobody can request one on your behalf.

## Request an inspection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the responsibility">
    Go to **Your Stuff → As Employee → Your Responsibilities** and select **Prepare** on the responsibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the request">
    Select **Request Inspection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the level and the Coach">
    Choose the badge level you're aiming for, then pick one Coach from the list. Only people who currently hold a Coach badge on this responsibility are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Select **Make Request**. The Coach you chose sees your request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can have one open request per responsibility at a time.

<Note>
  If nobody can inspect you yet, you see **No coach is currently available for this Responsibility. Please contact your administrator.** Ask your administrator to appoint a Coach.
</Note>

You can't request an inspection from inside a checkpoint. A checkpoint that needs an inspection points you back to **Your Responsibilities**.

## Follow your requests

All your requests are on **Your Stuff → As Employee → Your Inspection Requests**. Each row shows the responsibility, your level when you asked, the level you asked for, the date, the Coach, and the status.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Waiting for the Coach to accept. |
| **Accepted** | The Coach accepted and will inspect you. |
| **Completed** | You were inspected and earned a new badge. |
| **Declined** | The Coach couldn't take it. The reason is shown. You can ask another Coach. |
| **Failed** | You didn't meet the standard this time. The reason is shown. |
| **Canceled** | You or an administrator canceled it. |

Select **Prepare** on a pending or accepted row to go back to the responsibility's checklist while you wait.

## Cancel a request

On a pending or accepted row, select **Cancel**. If the Coach had already accepted, they're told you canceled.

On a declined or failed row, **Cancel** clears it from your list, so you can make a new request.

## Try again after a failed inspection

On the responsibility's Prepare view, a failed request shows the Coach's reason and a **Retry** button. Work on what the Coach pointed out, then select **Retry** to send a new request to the same Coach. If that Coach can no longer inspect, choose another with **Request Inspection**.
