## Readiness is more than training

Training gives you knowledge, and that's only the start. You're ready for a responsibility when you have all three:

| Part | What it means | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Knowledge** | You know what to do. | Training covers this part. |
| **Ability** | You can do it. | |
| **Behavior** | You actually do it, every day. | |

exIQtive.com checks all three, one responsibility at a time. See [Readiness concepts](/concepts/readiness).

## 1. Know your roles and responsibilities

Your position comes with roles, and each role with responsibilities. Together they show exactly what your job involves and what you're accountable for. For example, a Machine Operator might be responsible for operating the press safely, completing daily equipment checks, reporting quality issues and keeping the work area clean.

You see all of yours in [Your Stuff](/employees/your-stuff), so there's no guessing what good looks like.

## 2. Prepare and prove it

Each responsibility has a checklist of checkpoints. You prepare at your own pace with the short videos and articles on them, available online at any time. Then a short quiz, a knowledge check, proves you've retained what you learned. For many checkpoints, a Coach then inspects you doing the work. See [Prepare on a responsibility](/employees/prepare-on-a-responsibility) and [Request an inspection](/employees/request-an-inspection).

## 3. Grow your readiness level

As you prove your readiness, you move up the scale for each responsibility:

| Level | Readiness | Zone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Novice | 0% | Developing |
| Apprentice | 50% | Developing |
| Proficient | 100% | Competent |
| Coach | 125% | Expert |
| Architect | 150% | Expert |

The expected level is **Competent**: Proficient, the green band. On some responsibilities you can choose to go further and become an expert, as a Coach or an Architect. See [The Badge Ladder](/user-guide/readiness/badge-ladder).

## Earn your badges and keep them current

Each level you reach on a responsibility is a badge. Every badge is a **micro performance evaluation**: proof that you can do one specific part of your job, earned by being evaluated on the job.

You're expected to earn your badges and keep them current, so your record always shows what you can do today. Your badges add up to your overall Readiness. See [How Readiness is calculated](/user-guide/readiness/how-readiness-is-calculated).

## Know. Prove. Execute.

Know your role, prove your readiness, and execute every day. Ready to start? Follow the [Employee quickstart](/get-started/employee-quickstart).
